Turns a completed audit working file, kick-off deck and notification into a formatted draft Word report. Opportunity Radar12-stage pipeline→

Scans startup activity, abstracts each company into a transferable pattern, then grades it against a national policy corpus. pgvector retrieval pulls top-k evidence per document type, a knockout filter kills saturated or already-implemented ideas, and an eight-criterion weighted rubric scores what survives — with a confidence band derived from how much of the score is actually evidence-backed.
